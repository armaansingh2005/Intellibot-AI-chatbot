# Community standards

We aim to keep participation in this educational project welcoming, respectful, and focused on constructive collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate when discussing ideas and reviewing contributions.
- Offer actionable feedback about the work, not personal attacks.
- Respect differing backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Protect privacy: do not publish secrets, personal information, or real customer conversations.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports. The owner should review reports fairly and may remove content or restrict participation when necessary.

This policy applies to project spaces and when someone is representing the project.
